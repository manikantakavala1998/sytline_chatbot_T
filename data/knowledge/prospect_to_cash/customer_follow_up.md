# Customer Follow-up and Relationship Management

## Follow-up across the process

Follow-up starts before an order and continues after payment. Use Sales Contact Interactions, Prospect Interactions, Customer Interactions, Opportunity Tasks, and the site's communication tools to record the customer's question, agreed action, owner, and due date. An interaction is a communication record; an order, invoice, credit memo, or payment must still be checked in its owning form.

**Section Summary:** Record conversations in CRM, then verify operational facts in orders and A/R.

### Keywords
customer follow-up, Customer Interactions, Sales Contact Interactions, Opportunity Tasks

## Follow up after a quote

1. Locate the opportunity and estimate; confirm recipient, quote date, expiration, and latest version.
2. Record when and how the quote was sent; note requested changes and the next contact date.
3. Update Opportunity Tasks and opportunity stage/status according to local definitions.
4. If accepted, validate customer setup and create/check the order. If rejected, record the lost reason and close or revise the opportunity under local practice.

**Section Summary:** Quote follow-up needs a recorded customer response and a separate order or loss decision.

### Keywords
quote follow-up, quotation acceptance, opportunity task, lost reason

## Follow up after shipment and invoice

Identify the customer, order, shipment, and invoice before answering a delivery or billing concern. Compare ordered, shipped, and invoiced quantities; check ship date, freight/carrier details, invoice hold, and any credit memo or RMA. Record the issue and owner in Customer Interactions or the local case-management tool. A general knowledge article cannot provide a customer's actual shipment or invoice status.

**Section Summary:** Customer service should trace order, shipment, invoice, and return evidence together.

### Keywords
delivery follow-up, invoice follow-up, shipped quantity, complaint

## Collection follow-up

Use A/R Aging Report with the intended as-of date and inspect invoice-level open items. Check payment application, credits, disputes, and agreed terms before contacting the customer. Record the communication and escalate a disputed invoice through the site's A/R procedure. Do not assert that a customer is overdue based on a static document.

**Section Summary:** Collection follow-up depends on current A/R detail and payment application.

### Keywords
collections, overdue follow-up, payment reminder, disputed invoice

## Interaction record checklist

For any prospect or customer contact, capture the date, channel, contact person, related prospect/customer and transaction number, a factual summary of the request, action owner, promised next step, due date, and outcome. Use the appropriate interaction or task form rather than placing the only record in a private chat. Do not copy payment card details, credentials, or unnecessary personal information into notes. The organization's retention and case-routing rules are **[NEEDS SYTELINE CONFIRMATION]**.

**Section Summary:** Follow-up is complete when the conversation and accountable next action are recorded.

### Keywords
interaction fields, follow-up owner, contact date, next action, customer history

## Example follow-up questions by stage

| Stage | Question to resolve | Record to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | Is the customer interested and who will contact them next? | Lead, sales contact, prospect interaction. |
| Opportunity | What decision or task is blocking progress? | Opportunity status/stage and open tasks. |
| Quote | Was the current version sent and when does it expire? | Estimate and communication record. |
| Order | Was the requested quantity and date entered correctly? | Customer Order header and line. |
| Shipment | What quantity actually shipped and when? | Shipping transaction and packing reference. |
| Invoice | Was the shipment billed and for how much? | Invoice history and A/R item. |
| Payment | What remains open after receipts and credits? | Posted A/R payment distribution and open item. |

**Section Summary:** Each follow-up question has an owning transaction or CRM record.

### Keywords
customer follow-up by stage, quote, order, shipment, invoice, payment
