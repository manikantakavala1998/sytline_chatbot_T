# Customer Follow-up and Relationship Management

## Follow-up across the process

Follow-up starts before an order and continues after payment. Use Sales Contact Interactions, Prospect Interactions, Customer Interactions, Opportunity Tasks, and the site's communication tools to record the customer's question, agreed action, owner, and due date. An interaction is a communication record; an order, invoice, credit memo, or payment must still be checked in its owning form. [Infor CRM overview](https://docs.infor.com/csi/10.x/en-us/csbiolh/sales_crm_user_cl_sl/mergedprojects/sl_custvend/other/overview/crm_overview.html)

**Section Summary:** Record conversations in CRM, then verify operational facts in orders and A/R.

### Keywords
customer follow-up, Customer Interactions, Sales Contact Interactions, Opportunity Tasks

## Follow up after a quote

1. Locate the opportunity and estimate; confirm recipient, quote date, expiration, and latest version.
2. Record when and how the quote was sent; note requested changes and the next contact date.
3. Update Opportunity Tasks and opportunity stage/status according to local definitions.
4. If accepted, validate customer setup and create/check the order. If rejected, record the lost reason and close or revise the opportunity under local practice. [Infor CRM scenario 3](https://docs.infor.com/csi/2026.x/en-us/csbiolh/sales_crm_user_cl_sl/lsm1454144070779.html) [Infor Opportunities form](https://docs.infor.com/csi/9.01.x/en-us/csbiolh/mergedprojects/sl_custvend/forms/crmtopics/opportunities.htm)

**Section Summary:** Quote follow-up needs a recorded customer response and a separate order or loss decision.

### Keywords
quote follow-up, quotation acceptance, opportunity task, lost reason

## Follow up after shipment and invoice

Identify the customer, order, shipment, and invoice before answering a delivery or billing concern. Compare ordered, shipped, and invoiced quantities; check ship date, freight/carrier details, invoice hold, and any credit memo or RMA. Record the issue and owner in Customer Interactions or the local case-management tool. A general knowledge article cannot provide a customer's actual shipment or invoice status. [Infor shipping customer orders](https://docs.infor.com/csi/2026.x/en-us/csbiolh/customer_svc_user_cl_sl/lsm1454144032599.html) [Infor order invoicing](https://docs.infor.com/csi/10.x/en-us/csbiolh/customer_svc_user_cl_sl/lsm1454144031725.html)

**Section Summary:** Customer service should trace order, shipment, invoice, and return evidence together.

### Keywords
delivery follow-up, invoice follow-up, shipped quantity, complaint

## Collection follow-up

Use A/R Aging Report with the intended as-of date and inspect invoice-level open items. Check payment application, credits, disputes, and agreed terms before contacting the customer. Record the communication and escalate a disputed invoice through the site's A/R procedure. Do not assert that a customer is overdue based on a static document. [Infor A/R Aging Report](https://docs.infor.com/csi/latest/en-us/csbiolh/financials_user_cl_sl/lsm1454143885762.html) [Infor accounts receivable steps](https://docs.infor.com/csi/10.x/en-us/csbiolh/financials_user_cl_sl/lsm1454143881752.html)

**Section Summary:** Collection follow-up depends on current A/R detail and payment application.

### Keywords
collections, overdue follow-up, payment reminder, disputed invoice
