# Lead Module

## Record meaning and form fields

The Leads form records sales interest for either a prospect or an existing customer. It supports source, quality, status, notes, and links to opportunities, estimates, orders, and projects. Customer and Prospect are mutually exclusive selections on the form. A lead remains a lead after an opportunity is created; the opportunity number becomes visible on the lead. Status labels and the decision to advance are organization-defined.

**Section Summary:** A lead tracks interest and qualification; its opportunity is a linked record, not a replacement.

### Keywords
lead, Leads form, lead source, lead quality, lead status

## Capture and progress a lead

1. Confirm whether the interested organization is a Prospect or Customer; select the correct one.
2. Use a configured Lead Status. Record the source and quality if your site uses them.
3. Link the relevant sales contact, record the need and product or service interest in notes, and set a follow-up through Prospect Interactions where appropriate.
4. Assign the salesperson or owner under local practice, then review open leads regularly.
5. When qualified, open Opportunities from the Leads form and create a connected opportunity; retain the lead as the original interest record.

**Section Summary:** Capture the party, need, owner, status, and next contact before creating an opportunity.

### Keywords
create lead, qualify lead, follow-up date, prospect interaction, convert lead to opportunity

## What to review when a lead stalls

Check the lead's latest interaction and next follow-up date; confirm the contact and party references; review whether an opportunity already exists; and inspect the configured status meaning. A lead's status does not by itself prove a quote was sent, an order was booked, or revenue was recognized. The related Estimates and Orders tabs can help trace later records.

**Section Summary:** Use linked records to distinguish interest, proposal, and booked order.

### Keywords
stalled lead, follow-up, related opportunity, related order

## Lead field guide and qualification decision

| Field or group | Meaning | Check before moving forward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead identifier | Unique interest record | Confirm the record being discussed, especially when one account has several leads. |
| Prospect or Customer | Organization attached to the lead | Only one party type is chosen on the standard form. |
| Sales contact | Person who expressed interest | Confirm the contact is linked to the selected organization. |
| Source | How the interest arrived | Use the site's configured source values for reporting. |
| Quality and Status | Assessment and current lead state | Their labels are locally configured; ask what the site's values mean. |
| Notes and interactions | Need, objections, follow-up | Preserve the context for the opportunity owner. |

A lead becomes worth pursuing when there is a real business need, an identifiable party/contact, a responsible salesperson, and an agreed next action. Those are practical qualification questions, not universal SyteLine validation rules. An opportunity may be opened from the lead; the lead remains as history.

**Section Summary:** Qualification depends on the party, need, owner, and next action; status labels are local.

### Keywords
lead fields, lead qualification, lead owner, source, quality, status

## Handoff and exception examples

If the lead is for an existing customer, select Customer rather than creating a new Prospect. If several people at the same company inquire, decide whether they represent one buying project or separate leads before creating duplicates. If the user asks “What happened to this lead?”, inspect related opportunity, estimate, and order tabs; a lead status alone cannot prove a quote or order was completed. If a requested status is unavailable, verify Lead Statuses setup and the user's permissions with the application administrator.

**Section Summary:** Related records show progress beyond the lead, while missing values require setup or security review.

### Keywords
existing customer lead, duplicate lead, linked estimate, Lead Statuses
