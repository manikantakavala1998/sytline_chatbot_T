# Lead Module

## Record meaning and form fields

The Leads form records sales interest for either a prospect or an existing customer. It supports source, quality, status, notes, and links to opportunities, estimates, orders, and projects. Customer and Prospect are mutually exclusive selections on the form. A lead remains a lead after an opportunity is created; the opportunity number becomes visible on the lead. Status labels and the decision to advance are organization-defined. [Infor Leads form](https://docs.infor.com/csi/9.01.x/en-us/csbiolh/mergedprojects/sl_custvend/forms/crmtopics/leads.htm)

**Section Summary:** A lead tracks interest and qualification; its opportunity is a linked record, not a replacement.

### Keywords
lead, Leads form, lead source, lead quality, lead status

## Capture and progress a lead

1. Confirm whether the interested organization is a Prospect or Customer; select the correct one.
2. Use a configured Lead Status. Record the source and quality if your site uses them.
3. Link the relevant sales contact, record the need and product or service interest in notes, and set a follow-up through Prospect Interactions where appropriate.
4. Assign the salesperson or owner under local practice, then review open leads regularly.
5. When qualified, open Opportunities from the Leads form and create a connected opportunity; retain the lead as the original interest record. ## What to review when a lead stalls

Check the lead's latest interaction and next follow-up date; confirm the contact and party references; review whether an opportunity already exists; and inspect the configured status meaning. A lead's status does not by itself prove a quote was sent, an order was booked, or revenue was recognized. The related Estimates and Orders tabs can help trace later records. **Section Summary:** Use linked records to distinguish interest, proposal, and booked order.
