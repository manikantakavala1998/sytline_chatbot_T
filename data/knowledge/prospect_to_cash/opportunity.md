# Opportunity Module

## Record meaning and form map

An opportunity represents a potential sale for a prospect or customer. The Opportunities form holds the party, salesperson or contact, status, stage, estimated value, chance of winning, projected close date, and won or lost reason. Opportunity Tasks holds agreed actions. Estimates, Orders, Competitors, and Team Members appear through related forms and tabs. These are pipeline forecasts, not posted revenue.

**Section Summary:** Opportunity is the sales pipeline record; tasks and estimates make the pursuit actionable.

### Keywords
opportunity, Opportunities form, pipeline, projected close date, win probability

## Create and manage an opportunity

1. Ensure required Opportunity Statuses exist; configure sources, stages, task types, and win/loss reasons if used.
2. From a lead or Opportunities, create a new record; select a prospect or customer and assign a salesperson or contact.
3. Set the initial status, estimated value, win chance, and projected close date; save.
4. Create Opportunity Tasks with a type, owner, priority, due date, and optional mandatory indicator.
5. As the deal progresses, update stage, status, probability, and expected close date; record the reason when won or lost.
6. Link an estimate to produce a priced proposal. Review competitors and team members if those functions are used.

**Section Summary:** Keep status, probability, close date, tasks, and quote links current.

### Keywords
create opportunity, Opportunity Tasks, stage, status, estimated value, won lost

## Forecast and handoff

Sales Forecasts can include opportunities for a sales period and salesperson; forecast values can change while the forecast is Draft. A won opportunity is not automatically the same as a booked customer order: verify the customer, estimate, and order records separately. For a prospect win, the documented CRM scenario includes setting a won reason, moving the prospect to a customer, and verifying the customer.

**Section Summary:** Forecast, win, customer conversion, and order booking are distinct checkpoints.

### Keywords
sales forecast, won opportunity, customer conversion, order handoff

## Opportunity field and task interpretation

| Data | Meaning | Common mistake to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Estimated Value | Sales estimate for the potential deal | It is not an order total or an invoice. |
| Chance of winning | Probability assumption for forecasting | It is not a guarantee of acceptance. |
| Projected Close Date | Expected decision or close timing | It is not necessarily the shipment or invoice date. |
| Status and Stage | Configured sales progress labels | Their exact meanings must come from local setup. |
| Won/Lost reason | Outcome classification | Record it when the decision is known, not when a quote is merely sent. |
| Opportunity Task | Action, owner, priority, due date, completion | A completed task does not itself close the opportunity. |

Keep the opportunity linked to the correct prospect or customer. Use the related Estimates tab to find the proposal and Orders tab to check whether business was booked. Competitor and team-member relationships are maintained through their own cross-reference forms where enabled.

**Section Summary:** Pipeline estimates, task completion, and transactional order status are different evidence.

### Keywords
opportunity fields, estimated value, probability, projected close, Opportunity Tasks

## Review a stalled or inconsistent opportunity

First check the latest interaction and overdue tasks, then compare projected close date, status, stage, and estimate validity. If the deal is marked won but no order appears, confirm whether the customer was created and whether the estimate was copied or an order was entered separately. If the forecast looks wrong, inspect the selected sales period, salesperson, and forecast status before changing opportunity value. Preserve the reason for a loss or a changed expected value according to local policy.

**Section Summary:** Trace the quote and order rather than treating “won” as proof of booking.

### Keywords
stalled opportunity, won without order, expired estimate, forecast mismatch
