# Opportunity Module

## Record meaning and form map

An opportunity represents a potential sale for a prospect or customer. The Opportunities form holds the party, salesperson or contact, status, stage, estimated value, chance of winning, projected close date, and won or lost reason. Opportunity Tasks holds agreed actions. Estimates, Orders, Competitors, and Team Members appear through related forms and tabs. These are pipeline forecasts, not posted revenue. [Infor Opportunities form](https://docs.infor.com/csi/9.01.x/en-us/csbiolh/mergedprojects/sl_custvend/forms/crmtopics/opportunities.htm)

**Section Summary:** Opportunity is the sales pipeline record; tasks and estimates make the pursuit actionable.

### Keywords
opportunity, Opportunities form, pipeline, projected close date, win probability

## Create and manage an opportunity

1. Ensure required Opportunity Statuses exist; configure sources, stages, task types, and win/loss reasons if used.
2. From a lead or Opportunities, create a new record; select a prospect or customer and assign a salesperson or contact.
3. Set the initial status, estimated value, win chance, and projected close date; save.
4. Create Opportunity Tasks with a type, owner, priority, due date, and optional mandatory indicator.
5. As the deal progresses, update stage, status, probability, and expected close date; record the reason when won or lost.
6. Link an estimate to produce a priced proposal. Review competitors and team members if those functions are used. ## Forecast and handoff

Sales Forecasts can include opportunities for a sales period and salesperson; forecast values can change while the forecast is Draft. A won opportunity is not automatically the same as a booked customer order: verify the customer, estimate, and order records separately. For a prospect win, the documented CRM scenario includes setting a won reason, moving the prospect to a customer, and verifying the customer.
