# Prospect-to-Cash Overview

## The End-to-End Flow

Prospect-to-Cash is the full sequence a sale moves through, start to finish:

1. **Prospect** — a potential customer, not yet qualified.

2. **Lead** — a Prospect qualified as genuinely interested.

3. **Opportunity** — a qualified potential sale being actively pursued.

4. **Estimate** — a preliminary price and terms calculation.

5. **Quotation** — a formal price document sent to the customer for acceptance.

6. **Customer** — created once a Quotation is accepted.

7. **Customer Order** — the confirmed purchase request, made up of Order Lines with Pricing and a
   Credit check applied.

8. **Shipment** — the physical delivery of the ordered goods.

9. **Invoice** — the billing document generated after shipment (or on release, for service lines).

10. **Payment** — the customer's remittance against one or more Invoices, reducing their
    Receivable balance.

**Section Summary:** Each stage feeds the next — a Prospect becomes a Lead, an Opportunity, an
Estimate, a Quotation, a Customer, a Customer Order, a Shipment, an Invoice, and finally a
Payment.
